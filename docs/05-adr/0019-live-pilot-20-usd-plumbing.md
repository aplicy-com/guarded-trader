# ADR-0019: $20 live plumbing pilot on Kraken, before Gate 2

## Status

Accepted — 2026-10-05, by the operator. Amends, for the pilot only: the Phase 4 entry
gate and the 3A "VPS running ≥ 7 days" exit item in [`docs/07-roadmap.md`](../07-roadmap.md) /
[`docs/phase-3-plan.md`](../phase-3-plan.md); R1's "one $100 deposit" wording in
[`docs/02-requirements.md`](../02-requirements.md); and the strategy-promotion ladder in
`CLAUDE.md` §4 (the operator overrides it in writing for this pilot, see "Explicit overrides").
Everything else in those documents stands. ADR-0018 (fail-closed boot) applies unchanged.

Amended 2026-10-05, before merge (decisions 11 and 12): the live stop-loss is placed **on the
exchange**, and backups are **namespaced by trading mode**. Both close gaps this ADR first
recorded as known.

## Context and problem statement

Three weeks of dry-run and the 3A drills validated the system against a *simulated* exchange.
Nothing yet proves the parts that only exist against the real one: that the API key's scopes and
IP whitelist work from the VPS, that real orders are placed, filled, cancelled and ingested
correctly, that Kraken's minimums and fee handling behave as the market-facts script says, that
the kill switch and nuclear protocol work with real orders, and that R5 account isolation holds
on the operator's real, non-empty account. Those can only be tested with real money, and the
cheapest way is a very small amount.

The strategy that is running, `Baseline4h`, is **known to have no edge** (see
`docs/research/03-baseline-backtest.md`): it is a plumbing test harness, not a candidate. The
pilot is therefore *not* an attempt to make money and must not be judged or scaled as one.

## Decision outcome

1. **A live pilot with a $20 principal**, funded once (≈ $28 CAD by Interac e-Transfer, converted
   to $20 USD on Kraken), running `Baseline4h` on Kraken spot, **one position at a time**.
2. **The remaining $80 stays undeposited and gated behind Gate 2**
   ([`08-paper-exit-criteria.md`](../08-paper-exit-criteria.md)): no further money goes in unless
   a strategy that passes Gate 2 exists *and* a superseding ADR says how the rest of the $100
   is added (TBD (Gate 2)). Until then there are no top-ups: the pilot's $20 is the whole
   principal of the pilot ledger, and R1 applies to it as written (one deposit, recorded once,
   immutable, no rescue deposits).
3. **Gate change.** Going live for the pilot requires a **72-hour clean soak counted from the
   2026-10-05 VPS rebuild** (15:29 UTC, `infra/live/soak-start`) instead of 7 days. "Clean" is
   what `conductor.cli soak-check` can prove from the dry-run ledger: ≥ 72 h elapsed, no halt
   raised by the system itself since the start, none active now. Conductor downtime is not in
   the ledger; the runbook has the operator check healthchecks.io's log. Human-engaged halts
   (the drills) do not count against it. The 72 h applies to the pilot only. Any later live
   stage keeps the original gates.
4. **A fresh live ledger.** The live stack uses its own Docker volume (`conductor-data-live`)
   and its own Freqtrade database (`tradesv3.live.sqlite`). The dry-run ledger is archived and
   never carried over, so simulated trades, equity history, fence totals and halts cannot
   contaminate R1/R2/R4 figures. `scripts/trading-mode.sh` refuses to go live if the live
   volume holds anything but an empty ledger or one whose principal is exactly 20.
5. **Pinned parameters.** `PRINCIPAL_USD=20`, `DAILY_PROFIT_CAP_USD=2`,
   `MAX_CONCURRENT_POSITIONS=1` are literals in `infra/docker-compose.live.yml`, reviewed in git;
   a stray `.env` value cannot change them. The R4 loss limits keep their drafts (3% / 10% /
   3 losses) and now mean $0.60 / $2.00 on $20.
6. **One position, five pairs.** Freqtrade's real stake minimum includes a stoploss reserve of up
   to 1.5× the exchange minimum. At the pilot's 19 USD stake (`available_capital`: 20 less a
   1 USD fee reserve, because Kraken charges the entry fee on top) every pair clears the minimum
   with ≥ 1.65× headroom; **two positions of 9.50 USD would not clear** it for SOL, LINK and
   LTC. Hence `max_open_trades: 1`
   ([`docs/research/04-live-pilot-sizing.md`](../research/04-live-pilot-sizing.md), regenerated by
   `make pilot-sizing`). The whitelist is BTC, XRP, SOL, LINK, LTC. **ETH/USD is removed** and
   every asset the operator already holds (ADA, ETH, DOT, SC, KAVA, ACA, XTZ, USDC) is
   blacklisted: R5.
7. **R5 becomes a mechanism, not just a check.** An append-only `isolation_exclusions` table,
   `conductor.cli isolation-check` and `exclude-balance`, the existing startup check now honouring
   exclusions, and a second check before Conductor starts any freshly booted Freqtrade in live
   mode (AC5.3 "before the first trade after any restart"). The clearance runbook (AC5.4) is
   `start-stop.md`, "R5 abort".
8. **Key containment and scope.** The Kraken key reaches the `freqtrade` container only (never
   `conductor`, never the dry-run container, not even under its raw name), via
   `infra/docker-compose.live.yml`. It is IP-whitelisted to the VPS (203.0.113.10) and has Query
   funds, Query open/closed orders, Create/modify orders and Cancel/close orders **only**: no
   withdraw, no deposit, no funding. A **query-only** key is created and tested first from the VPS;
   `scripts/kraken-access-check.sh` verifies scopes behaviourally (read, validate-only order,
   and "permission denied" on deposit and withdraw lookups; it never calls a withdraw).
9. **One switch, with gates that cannot be flagged away.** `scripts/trading-mode.sh live|dry-run`
   (also `make go-live` / `make go-dry-run`). Going live refuses unless: soak (above); key scopes
   verified now; live ledger fresh; dry-run ledger archived; Freqtrade reports `dry_run=false`
   and `paused`; R5 passes against the real balances. It takes no skip/force/duration arguments.
   Going back refuses while a real position or order is open or cannot be read.
   Freqtrade also never starts a position-opening state on its own: it boots `paused`
   (ADR-0018) and Conductor starts it.
10. **Mode is cross-checked.** Conductor refuses to start if Freqtrade's `dry_run` contradicts
    `TRADING_MODE` in either direction, and re-checks before every Freqtrade start. Telegram
    (status, help, every confirmation) and the dashboard show a `LIVE: REAL MONEY` banner.

11. **The stop lives on Kraken.** `config.live.json` sets `stoploss_on_exchange: true` with order
    type `market`, so an open position keeps a real stop-loss order on Kraken if Freqtrade, Conductor
    or the whole VPS is down. Verified against the pinned Freqtrade 2026.8:
    - Its Kraken class declares `stoploss_on_exchange: True` with `stoploss_order_types`
      `{limit, market}` (a unit test reads this from the image). `market` is Kraken's stop-loss
      order (triggers a market sell), chosen over stop-limit so it cannot be skipped over.
    - It works with `Baseline4h`'s `custom_stoploss`. Observed in a scratch dry-run bot (which runs
      Freqtrade's real stop-order code path against simulated orders): when the entry fills,
      Freqtrade places the stop at the *initial* stop (`stoploss`, -99%, i.e. almost nothing), then
      after `stoploss_on_exchange_interval` cancels it and places one at the custom ATR stop
      (about 1.8% below entry for the BTC trade tried). The ATR stop is fixed, so this happens once
      per trade. `stoploss_on_exchange_interval` is **10 s** (default 60) to keep the window with
      only the wide stop short.
    - Pausing (`/kill`) leaves the stop; `forceexit` (the nuclear protocol) cancels it and sells;
      Freqtrade's shutdown does not cancel it (`cancel_open_orders_on_exit` is false).
    - The stop is **live only**: the dry-run config is unchanged, so the soak run is not disturbed.
12. **Backups are namespaced by mode.** Keys are `backups/live/<source>/...` and
    `backups/dry_run/<source>/...` (the audit record carries the mode too). `restore-test.sh` takes
    `--mode live|dry_run|legacy` and has no default; `legacy` reads the older
    `backups/<source>/...` keys, which are dry-run backups. The restore and rebuild runbooks use the
    mode, the matching volume (`infra_conductor-data-live`) and trade DB (`tradesv3.live.sqlite`).

## Explicit overrides (the operator, 2026-10-05)

- **`CLAUDE.md` §4 promotion ladder** (backtest → walk-forward → 3-week paper → 25% → 100%) is
  not applied to this pilot: it is a plumbing test of a no-edge strategy at 20% of the planned
  principal, not a promotion. Nothing about the pilot promotes `Baseline4h` or counts toward Gate 2
  or Gate 3. The ladder applies in full to any real strategy.
- **Phase 4 entry gate** ("Phase 3 exit gate met") is not met and is not waived for anything
  beyond this pilot. The two Phase 4 TODOs that *block going live* (R5 mechanism, fresh live
  ledger) are done by this change; re-recording the `/balance` fixture from the live instance is
  part of the pilot's first-day checks.

## What this ADR does *not* change

- **Tax.** The accountant consultation (risk register T1/T4, `docs/09-tax-questions.md`) is not
  done and is not waived: pilot trades have tax consequences whose classification is
  `TBD (Phase 2)`. The ledger values every closed trade in CAD from the first trade (NFR6), so
  nothing is lost by classifying later; the runbook makes the operator tick this consciously
  before funding.
- ADR-0003 (the LLM never touches orders), NFR2 (no withdrawal capability), R3 (profit is
  fenced in the ledger; the physical move is manual), R4, R6, R7.

## Consequences

- **Real orders will be placed**, by Freqtrade only, on a no-edge strategy. Expect to lose a little
  to fees and spread: the $20 is the price of the plumbing test. Max realistic loss is the
  principal.
- **Stop limits (decision 11), what it does and does not give you.** If the VPS is down, an open
  position still has a stop on Kraken at the ATR level, and it executes as a market sell with
  normal slippage. It does **not** run the strategy's exit signals, Conductor's halts or
  Freqtrade's protections while the bot is down. For roughly the first 10 s after an entry fills
  the exchange holds only the wide initial stop (software stops still apply if the bot is up). The
  stop reserves the coin on Kraken, so Freqtrade cancels it before any other exit order (it does
  this itself; a failure to re-create it is logged and Freqtrade force-exits). A stop-loss order is
  still an order: Kraken must accept it, and the first-day check confirms it appears.
- **Backups (decision 12)** are separated, so a restore can never return the other mode's ledger.
  The `legacy` mode exists only until the first nightly run after this is deployed.
- **Known gap: fencing vs. stake.** Freqtrade sizes from `available_capital` plus its own closed
  profit and does not know about fenced profit; Conductor's existing `capital_exceeded` halt (R2
  second layer) is what catches an over-stake. At a $2/day cap on a $19 stake this is a theoretical
  gap, recorded rather than ignored.
- **Pre-existing USD on the account** is not whitelisted-asset exposure so R5's balance check does
  not see it; sizing is bounded by `available_capital`, not by the account's USD balance.
- The 72 h soak window contains drills 1, 3 and 4 (human-driven halts, a 5 min 42 s Conductor
  outage). They are accepted under "clean" as defined above; move `infra/live/soak-start` later
  to exclude them.
- Scaling beyond $20 needs a new ADR and Gate 2; "the pilot went fine" is not that.
